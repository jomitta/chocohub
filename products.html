Heritage & Passion

The COCO Journey

Founded in 2021 by master chocolatier and pastry chef Elena Vance, COCO Café & Chocolate Boutique started with a single vision: to elevate everyday desserts into unforgettable sensory experiences.

Unlike mass-produced commercial confectioneries, every single cake tier, batch of tapioca pearls for our signature brown sugar bubble tea, molten lava cake batter, French macaron shell, and artisan truffle is prepared by hand using time-honored artisanal techniques.

We source our cacao exclusively from sustainable, fair-trade farms across Ecuador and Madagascar, pairing them with fresh local dairy and premium ingredients. Whether you're celebrating a life milestone with one of our custom cakes or enjoying a relaxing afternoon with truffle loaded fries and a refreshing boba, COCO welcomes you into our sweet family.
